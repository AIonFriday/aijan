          Questions

          Good to know

          Is it really free?

          Yes. Every session is free, all month. There’s nothing to buy and no sales pitch.

          I’ve never used AI. Is this for me?

          Yes. Lots of sessions are for people who have never tried it. On the Sessions page, pick “New to AI” under Level to see those.

          What happens at a session?

          A small group, a table, and a Guide who uses AI every day. They show you what they do on a laptop, then you try it with help. No slides. Most sessions are 90 minutes. Office hours are two hours, and you can drop in any time.

          Where and when?

          Weekdays from Jan 8 to 29, 2027, mostly at The Nieux, 2040 St. Charles Ave. Sessions are in the morning, at lunch, or in the afternoon. Each session’s page has its time and place. How to get there.

          How do I sign up?

          Tap RSVP on a session and give us your email. That’s it: no account and no password. We’ll email you if anything changes.

          Why isn’t the whole month on the calendar yet?

          On purpose. A few sessions are set, and we build the rest around what people ask for. Tap “I want this” on any idea you’d come to, or suggest your own. When five people want one, we find a Guide, put it on the calendar, and email everyone who asked.

          Can my team or group have its own session?

          Yes. Bring your krewe: your office, congregation, class, or block. We’ll match you with a Guide and a time.

          I use AI. Can I lead a session?

          Yes, please. We’re looking for more faculty. If you use AI most days, you’re further along than you think, and you can really help people getting started. You don’t need slides: open your laptop and show what you do. Offer to guide a session and we’ll help with the rest.

          What should I bring?

          A laptop if you have one. A phone works too. If you have a project or an idea you want to get done, bring that.

          Can I sponsor or donate?

          To help cover food and drinks, email tim@nieux.co. If you make software and want to offer trials to people who come, email andrew@aifri.day.
