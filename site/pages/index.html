      Questions

      Good to know

      Is it really free?

      Yes. Every session is free, all month.

      Do I need to know anything about AI?

      No. Lots of sessions are for people who have never tried it. On the Sessions page, pick “New to AI” under Level to see those. We’re here to help you learn!

      What should I bring?

      If you want to do something hands-on, bring a laptop if you can, but a phone works too. If you have something you’re working on, or an idea for a project you want to get done, bring that too!

      Where and when?

      Weekdays from Jan 8 to 29, 2027, at The Nieux, 2040 St. Charles Ave.

      Why don’t some sessions have a date yet?

      Some sessions are only proposed so far. Once a few people say they’re interested, we’ll find the right person to help facilitate and get it on the schedule. Pick the sessions you’re interested in, or propose a new session, and we’ll find someone.

      How does a session get on the calendar?

      Anyone can suggest one. We read every idea, post the good ones, and once five people say “I’m in” and a guide signs up, we schedule it and email everyone.

      Can my team or group have its own session?

      Yes. Bring your krewe and we’ll match you with a guide and a time.

      I use AI. Can I help?

      Yes! Even if you don’t consider yourself an expert, if you’re using it daily you’re a lot further ahead than many others, and you can be a real help to people getting started.

      If you’d like to guide a session on a particular topic, please reach out to andrew@aifri.day and we’ll get you on the schedule! You don’t need a formal presentation. Cracking open a laptop and showing others what you’re doing makes an excellent one.

      Can I sponsor or donate?

      If you’re interested in sponsoring or donating funds to help cover the cost of food or refreshments, contact tim@nieux.co.

      If you’re a software vendor that would like to feature your software or offer trials to attendees, contact andrew@aifri.day.
