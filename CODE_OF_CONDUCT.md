# Code of conduct

AI January exists so that anyone in New Orleans can learn AI without feeling out of place. That goes for this repo, the sessions, and every space connected to them.

**We expect everyone to:**

- Be welcoming, especially to people who are new to AI or new to code. There are no dumb questions.
- Be patient and kind in reviews and comments. Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

**Not okay:**

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments of any kind, including about someone's identity, background, age, or skill level.
- Sharing someone's personal information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or sustained disruption.

**If something happens,** contact the organizers through [aifri.day](https://aifri.day/) or by messaging a maintainer of this repo. We'll keep it confidential as far as we can. Organizers may remove comments, close pull requests, or remove people from the project or the event.
